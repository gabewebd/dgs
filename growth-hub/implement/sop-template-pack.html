SOP Template Pack

Turn tribal knowledge into repeatable systems your team can actually follow. Use this template pack to document critical workflows, clarify ownership, standardize execution, and identify opportunities for improvement or automation.

The fastest-growing businesses do not rely on memory, individual workarounds, or "the way we usually do it." This pack walks you from choosing which process is actually worth documenting, through writing it, assigning ownership, finding the friction, and keeping it current as work changes.

This is not

A single generic SOP template nobody customizes.

This is

5 templates plus self-assessments for choosing, writing, owning, and improving your SOPs.

What's Inside

01

Before You Write an SOP

A quick worksheet to identify which processes are actually worth documenting first.

02

SOP Master Template

The primary end-to-end template for documenting a complete recurring workflow.

03

One-Page SOP

A lightweight version for simple, repeatable tasks that don't need the full template.

04

Role & Responsibility Template

Who performs, reviews, and approves each step, plus a handoff check between people or teams.

05

Process Improvement Template

06

Keep documentation current whenever a process changes, not just when someone remembers to.

07

A final pass across clarity, ownership, consistency, and maintenance before publishing.

09

A simple worksheet for choosing and starting the very first process to document.

How It Works

Not 10 templates to fill out at once. Here's the order to actually use them in.

01

Pick a process

Use the Priority Check to find one process worth documenting first, not the whole business at once.

02

Document it end to end

Fill out the Master Template for complex workflows, or the One-Page SOP for simple, repeatable tasks.

03

Assign ownership

Use the Role & Responsibility template so nobody has to guess who owns what.

04

Publish, then keep it current

Run the Quality Checklist before sharing it, and revisit it with the Review template whenever the process changes.
