AI Systems Engineer

Build custom AI agent pipelines and workflow automations connecting enterprise CRMs and operational databases.

What you'll do

  • Design and deploy AI agent pipelines that connect CRMs, databases, and internal tools
  • Build and maintain workflow automations across client operating systems
  • Partner with the automation team to translate business processes into reliable, monitored systems
  • Debug and optimize existing integrations as client stacks evolve

What we're looking for

  • Experience building automations with tools like n8n, Make, Zapier, or custom API integrations
  • Comfortable working directly with REST APIs, webhooks, and structured data
  • Strong problem-solving instincts and a bias toward shipping working systems over perfect ones
  • Clear written communication for a fully remote, async-friendly team
